How to choose

  • Breakout EAs trade fewer, session- or volatility-based moves and accept many small losses from false breaks.
  • Trend-following EAs aim to stay in larger moves and trail the stop; they struggle in ranges.
  • Mean-reversion EAs trade back toward an average in quiet markets and stand aside in strong trends.

Running two different styles can spread trades across market conditions - use a different magic number for each. Test any EA in the Strategy Tester with your broker's data, then on a demo account. Related: best timeframe for gold trading, the 1% risk rule, XAUUSD lot size calculator.

Gold EA FAQ

What is a gold EA?

A gold EA (Expert Advisor) is a program for MetaTrader that opens, manages and closes XAUUSD trades automatically from fixed rules, such as a breakout of the Asian range or a pullback in a trend.

Do these XAUUSD EAs work on MT4?

No. GoldLab EAs are built for MetaTrader 5 (.ex5) only. Any MT5 broker that offers XAUUSD works, on a hedging or netting account.

Do GoldLab gold EAs use martingale or grid?

No. Each trade has its own stop-loss, the lot size comes from your risk % or a fixed lot, and a daily loss limit stops trading for the day.

Can I use a gold EA on a prop firm account?

The fixed stop-loss and daily loss limit make it possible to set the EA inside common prop firm rules, but every firm has its own rules on EAs, news trading and drawdown. Check them first and test on the firm's demo. No EA can guarantee passing a challenge.

Which timeframe is best for a gold EA?

It depends on the strategy: scalping and session breakouts use M1 to M15, most trend and reversion EAs here use H1, and swing systems use H4. Each EA page lists its recommended timeframe.

Is there a free gold EA demo?

Yes. MQL5 Market products have a free demo you can run in the MT5 Strategy Tester on your broker's own XAUUSD data before buying.

Do I need a VPS to run an EA?

An EA only trades while MetaTrader 5 is open and connected, so a VPS close to your broker's server is recommended for 24/5 running.
