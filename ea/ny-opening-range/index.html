GoldLab NY Opening Range is a fully automated Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5. It is designed for XAUUSD (gold) and also runs on other symbols such as EURUSD.

How it trades

  1. Opening range - the high and low of the first N minutes (default 30) of the New York session, starting at the NY session start time you set in server time (default 13:30 - set 16:30 on a GMT+3 broker).
  2. Range filter - the range width must be between a minimum and maximum multiple of the H1 ATR.
  3. Breakout on a closed bar - on the signal timeframe (default M5) a bar must close beyond the range (plus a buffer); wicks alone do not trigger.
  4. Tick-volume filter - the breakout bar's tick volume must be at least the recent average (multiplier adjustable), to avoid low-participation breaks.
  5. Optional retest mode - instead of entering at the breakout, the EA waits for price to come back to the broken level and close back in the breakout direction within a set number of bars. A close back through the other side of the range cancels the setup.
  6. Exits - stop-loss at the opposite side of the range (or the range middle), take-profit at an R-multiple (default 1.5R), optional break-even, and a time exit that closes everything at the set hour (default 20:00 server time).
  7. Entries are allowed only inside the entry window after the range (default 180 minutes), with a maximum number of trades per day.

Risk management (built in)

  • Every trade is opened with a stop-loss. There is no martingale, no grid and no averaging down.
  • Position size from risk % of balance (default 1%) or a fixed lot; lots are normalised to the broker's min / max / step and reduced if free margin is not enough.
  • Daily loss limit: no new trades for the rest of the day once equity is down the set % from the day-start balance.
  • Max spread filter, optional trading-hours filter, and no orders while the market is closed or trading on the symbol is disabled.
  • Stop-loss / take-profit are always placed outside the broker's stops and freeze levels.
  • Works on hedging and netting accounts (on netting accounts it opens only when the symbol has no position).

FAQ

What is the difference between the London Breakout and NY Opening Range EAs?

London Breakout trades the range built during the Asian session as London opens. NY Opening Range measures a short range at the New York open and trades its breakout or retest, so the two cover different sessions.

Is there a free demo?

Yes. Every MQL5 Market product has a free demo that runs in the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, so you can test it on your broker's own data before buying.

Does it use martingale, grid or averaging down?

No. Every position is opened with a stop-loss, the lot size comes from your risk % (or a fixed lot), and there is a daily loss limit.

Which broker and account do I need?

Any MetaTrader 5 broker that offers XAUUSD, on a hedging or netting account. A low-spread account and a VPS for 24/5 operation are recommended.

Can this EA guarantee profits?

No. No trading system wins every trade, and backtest or past results do not guarantee future results. Start on a demo account with the default risk.
