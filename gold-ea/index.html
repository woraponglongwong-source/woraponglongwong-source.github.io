FAQ

How do I install an EA from the MQL5 Market?

Buy or rent it on MQL5 with the same MQL5 account that is logged in to your MetaTrader 5, then open the Market tab in MT5 → Purchased and click Install. It appears under Expert Advisors in the Navigator.

Which broker or account do I need?

Any MT5 broker that offers XAUUSD. A low-spread account and a VPS for 24/5 operation are recommended.

Can an EA guarantee profits?

No. No trading system wins every trade, and backtest results do not guarantee future results. Start on a demo account with the default risk.
