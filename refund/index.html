Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Because our products are digital downloads with a license key, refunds work as follows.

When you can get a refund

  • Within 7 days of purchase, license not activated: full refund, no questions asked.
  • The EA does not work as described (for example it will not load in MetaTrader 5 or a described feature is missing) and we cannot fix it within 7 days of your report: full refund.
  • Duplicate or accidental payment: full refund of the extra payment.

When refunds are not possible

  • Trading results: losses or lower-than-expected profit are not a reason for a refund. Trading is risky and no EA wins every trade.
  • Licenses that were activated on a live account and used for trading, after the 7-day window.
  • Licenses deactivated for sharing or misuse (see our Terms of Service).

How to request a refund

Contact us via our contact page with your order number and the email used at checkout. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; it can take 5–10 business days for your bank to show it. Your license key is deactivated when the refund is issued.
