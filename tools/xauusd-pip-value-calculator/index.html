XAUUSD pip value per lot

With the standard gold contract of 100 oz per lot and the common convention that 1 pip = 0.10 in price, one pip on XAUUSD is worth 10 USD per 1.00 lot, 1 USD per 0.10 lot and 0.10 USD per 0.01 lot. In MetaTrader the smallest price step - a point - is usually 0.01 on 2-digit gold quotes, worth 1 USD per lot.

Some websites call 0.01 a pip, others 1.00. That is why it is safer to think in price distance: a move from 4,200.00 to 4,210.00 is 10.00, worth 1,000 USD on 1 lot whatever you call the unit. Full explanation: XAUUSD pip value explained.

Forex pip values

  • Pairs quoted in USD (EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD): pip value = 100,000 × 0.0001 = 10 USD per lot, fixed.
  • Pairs with USD as the base (USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD): pip value = lots × 100,000 × pip size ÷ price. At USDJPY 150.00: 100,000 × 0.01 ÷ 150 = 6.67 USD per lot.
  • Cross pairs (EURJPY, EURGBP): convert the quote currency to USD. For JPY crosses enter the USDJPY rate, for EURGBP enter the GBPUSD rate - the calculator asks for the right one.

Worked examples

Gold. 0.25 lot XAUUSD, price moves 12.50 in your favour. Value = 0.25 × 100 × 12.50 = 312.50 USD (125 pips of 0.10 × 2.50 USD per pip).
Silver. XAGUSD usually has 5,000 oz per lot and a 0.01 pip, so 1 pip = 5,000 × 0.01 = 50 USD per lot. Check your broker's contract - some use 1,000 oz.
USDJPY. 0.50 lot at 150.00: 0.5 × 100,000 × 0.01 ÷ 150 = 3.33 USD per pip.

FAQ

How much is 1 pip on XAUUSD?

With a 100 oz contract and a pip of 0.10, one pip is worth 10 USD per 1.00 lot, 1 USD per 0.10 lot and 0.10 USD per 0.01 lot.

How much is a 1 dollar move in gold per lot?

A 1.00 USD move in the gold price is worth 100 USD on 1.00 lot, 10 USD on 0.10 lot and 1 USD on 0.01 lot with a standard 100 oz contract.

What is the difference between a pip and a point on gold?

A point is the smallest price step your broker quotes, usually 0.01 on gold. A pip is a convention, most often 0.10 on gold, so one pip equals ten points.

Does pip value change with my account currency?

Yes. The calculator shows USD. If your account is in EUR or another currency, the broker converts the USD amount at the current exchange rate.
