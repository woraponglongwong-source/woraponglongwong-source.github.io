How to calculate profit on XAUUSD

Gold is quoted in US dollars, so the profit of an XAUUSD trade is simply the price move multiplied by the ounces you hold. With the standard contract of 100 oz per 1.00 lot:

Profit (USD) = (Close − Open) × Lots × Contract size      for a buy
Profit (USD) = (Open − Close) × Lots × Contract size      for a sell

So every 1.00 USD the gold price moves is worth 100 USD on 1 lot, 10 USD on 0.10 lot and 1 USD on 0.01 lot. Subtract commission and swap to get the net result. Your broker may use a different contract size - check it in MetaTrader under XAUUSD → Specification → Contract size.

Worked examples

Buy, winning. 0.10 lot bought at 4,200.00, closed at 4,215.50. Move = +15.50. Profit = 15.50 × 0.10 × 100 = +155.00 USD (155 pips of 0.10, 1,550 points of 0.01).
Sell, winning. 0.50 lot sold at 4,230.00, closed at 4,218.00. Price fell 12.00, which is in your favour on a sell. Profit = 12.00 × 0.50 × 100 = +600.00 USD.
Buy, losing. 0.05 lot bought at 4,200.00, stopped out at 4,188.00. Move = −12.00. Result = −12.00 × 0.05 × 100 = −60.00 USD, before costs.

Gold pips to dollars

With 1 pip = 0.10 in price, one pip is worth 10 USD per 1.00 lot. That makes pip maths quick:

Lot10 pips50 pips100 pips500 pips
0.01$1$5$10$50
0.10$10$50$100$500
0.50$50$250$500$2,500
1.00$100$500$1,000$5,000

Some traders and websites call 0.01 a pip, which makes every number ten times bigger. If a figure looks off, compare price distance instead: 4,200.00 → 4,210.00 is a 10.00 move, worth 1,000 USD on 1 lot whatever the unit is called. Silver and forex

  • XAGUSD: usually 5,000 oz per lot. A move from 50.00 to 50.40 on 1 lot = 0.40 × 5,000 = 2,000 USD.
  • EURUSD / GBPUSD: 100,000 units per lot, so 1.0800 → 1.0850 on 1 lot = 0.0050 × 100,000 = 500 USD (50 pips).
  • USDJPY: profit is in yen first, then converted at the close price. 0.50 lot from 150.00 to 150.80 = 0.80 × 50,000 = 40,000 JPY ÷ 150.80 = 265.25 USD.

FAQ

How do I calculate profit on gold?

Multiply the price move by the lot size and the contract size. With 100 oz per lot, a 15.50 move on 0.10 lot is 15.50 × 0.10 × 100 = 155 USD. For a sell, the move counts as profit when the price falls.

How much profit is 100 pips on XAUUSD?

With 1 pip = 0.10, 100 pips is a 10.00 price move: 1,000 USD on 1.00 lot, 100 USD on 0.10 lot and 10 USD on 0.01 lot.

How much is a 1 dollar move in gold worth?

A 1.00 USD move is worth 100 USD on 1 standard lot (100 oz), 10 USD on 0.10 lot and 1 USD on 0.01 lot.

Does the calculator include spread?

Use real fill prices: a buy opens at the ask and closes at the bid, so the spread is already inside the open and close prices you enter. Add commission and swap in the cost field.

Why is my broker's profit slightly different?

Usually because of a different contract size, swap, commission, or an account in a currency other than USD - the broker converts the USD result at its own rate.
